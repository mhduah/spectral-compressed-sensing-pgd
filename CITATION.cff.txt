abstract: >
  Numerical implementation and experimental framework accompanying the
  MSc Mathematics thesis "Spectral Compressed Sensing via Projected
  Gradient Descent — Numerical Simulation and Analysis". The repository
  contains implementations of projected gradient descent (PGD), fast
  iterative hard thresholding (FIHT), atomic norm minimisation (ANM),
  EMaC/ADMM-based Hankel nuclear-norm recovery, and symmetric Hankel
  gradient descent (SHGD), together with experiment drivers, numerical
  result records, and scripts for generating the thesis figures.
